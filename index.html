How it works

  1. That day, on site: one extra = one ticket. Get it signed. Photograph the work and the signed ticket.
  2. The same week, in the office: enter the ticket in the Excel log.
  3. When you invoice: bill from the log. Attach the tickets and the photos. Do not bill an unsigned row as if it were approved.

FAQ

Is this a change order?

No. It is a field record of extra work. Some jobs require extras to be priced and approved in writing before you start. If your contract names a form, use that form.

Can I put my company name on it?

Yes. Write or stamp it on the PDF, or type it in the Excel Company cell.

Excel or Google Sheets?

Built for Microsoft Excel. Google Sheets will open the files; some formatting may shift. No macros.
