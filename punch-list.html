Who this is for

The problem

A punch list on a legal pad is how items come back after you left. The shops that close cleanly walk the job with the person who can accept it, write items a stranger could find, photograph the open ones, and carry Open and Disputed rows to the next walk.

How it works

  1. Walk it together. One walk = one list. Room + what is wrong, not “misc. touch-up.”
  2. Open items get a photo. Name files JOB#-WALK#-ITEM#.jpg.
  3. Enter the list in Excel the same day. Next walk starts from Open and Disputed rows, not a blank page.

FAQ

Is this a certificate of occupancy?

No. It is a field list. If the city or the architect has a required punch form, use that form.

What does Disputed mean?

Owner and contractor do not agree. Do not mark it Done. Leave it yellow until it is resolved in writing.

Excel or Google Sheets?

Built for Microsoft Excel. Google Sheets will open the files; some formatting may shift. No macros.
